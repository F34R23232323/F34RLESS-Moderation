## 1. About the Server

f34rless.hq is a community server built around clear standards: a **verified membership**, transparent rules, and a fully documented moderation system. Everyone is held to the same standards — staff included.

- **Verification** — a one-time website scan grants the Verified role. Opt out for privacy and request manual verification instead.
- **Zero-tolerance core** — hate speech, CSAM, doxxing, real-world threats, and malware/phishing are permanent-ban offences with no appeal rights.
- **6-month rolling record** — warnings expire after 6 clean months without further violations, and stop counting toward escalation.
- **Transparency** — every rule, enforcement tier, and AutoMod threshold is public and documented.
- **Staff standards** — every moderation action is logged, documented, and justified; staff conduct is held to a higher standard than member conduct.

The server is moderated by a **custom branded Sapphire bot** owned and operated by the Sapphire team under the **Infinite Limits subscription** — it runs with no feature or configuration limits. The server owner (f34rless.dev) uses the custom-branding feature to give the bot its own identity in this server. *(See [Bots & Affiliates](#11-links--resources--bots--affiliates).)*

## 2. Verification

Getting access to the rest of the server starts with a quick verification scan.

- **False flags** (VPN / proxy / shared household IP, incorrectly flagged alt) → open a ticket
- **Privacy concerns** (you don't want your IP scanned) → request manual verification
- **Technical issues** (site won't load, verified role not granted after a successful scan) → open a ticket

Open a ticket in the verification support channel for assistance.

**What NOT to open a ticket for:**
- Asking for a bypass simply because you don't feel like verifying.
- Complaining about or debating the server's security measures.
- General server questions (use the proper support channels once verified).

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Abusing the ticket system or opening troll support tickets may result in moderation action.

**Manual verification (privacy opt-out)** — you can opt out of the website scan for privacy reasons. Requests are usually granted if the account looks legitimate and established; they may be denied for highly suspicious accounts (brand new, missing avatar, bot-like profile) to protect the server from raiders.

```
4 identical / near-identical messages in 30s  →  Auto-delete + Tier 1 timeout
6 messages of any kind in 10s                 →  Auto-delete + Tier 1 timeout
10 messages in 30s                            →  Mute + staff alert
Repeated automod hits in one session          →  Escalates to Tier 2 warning
```

**Filter categories:**
> - **Slurs & hate language** — Auto-delete. Zero-tolerance. Staff alerted. Permanent ban follows confirmation.
> - **Threat language** — Auto-delete. Staff alert. Permanent ban if confirmed.
> - **Phishing & scam links** — Auto-delete. Staff alert. User muted pending review.
> - **Excessive emoji / sticker flooding** — Auto-delete after threshold. Tier 1 applied.
> - **All-caps flooding** — Auto-delete after 70% caps threshold in messages over 8 words.

### 5.2 Bypass Attempts

- Character substitution, zero-width characters, deliberate misspelling, or any other technique used to slip content past filters is classified as **intentional evasion**.
- Bypass attempts automatically increase the applicable case severity to the **maximum available penalty** — the filtered content is treated as if it had been posted successfully.
- There is no accidental-bypass defence. If you are deliberately circumventing a filter, you are deliberately violating the rule the filter enforces.

### 5.3 Escalation Table

```
1st automod hit (any category)   →  Auto-delete + logged
2nd hit same session             →  Auto-delete + Tier 1 timeout
3rd hit same session             →  Mute + staff review
4th hit or bypass attempt        →  Tier 2 warning + extended mute
Zero-tolerance content           →  Permanent ban + Trust & Safety report
```

All automod actions are logged and reviewable by senior staff. Staff may manually escalate beyond automated thresholds at any time without prior notice.

## 6. Enforcement Tiers

```
Tier 1  →  Verbal Reminder / Minor Timeout (1–5 min)
Tier 2  →  Formal Warning (expires in 6 months)
Tier 3  →  Temporary Mute (30 min – 24 hr)
Tier 4  →  Temporary Ban (1–30 days)
Tier 5  →  Permanent Ban (appealable for staff error)
Zero    →  Bypasses all tiers — instant Tier 5, no appeal
```

Staff may skip tiers upward based on severity. First offences may land at higher tiers if the conduct warrants it. Tier skipping is always documented.

### 6.1 Tier 1 — Deletion & Minor Timeout (1–5 minutes)

Reserved for isolated, low-level disruptions on a first occurrence. Repetition after a staff correction escalates to Tier 2.

- Spam or flooding — 4+ identical/near-identical messages within 30 seconds
- Excessive rapid emoji, sticker, or GIF use past the threshold
- Wrong channel posting with no prior correction on record
- Blank, filler, or meaningless messages that disrupt channel flow
- Missing spoiler tags on content that requires them
- Accidental staff ping with an immediate and genuine acknowledgment

**Outcome:** content deleted, 1–5 minute timeout, verbally noted. Second occurrence in the same session escalates to Tier 2.

### 6.2 Tier 2 — Formal Warning (6-month rolling)

Issued for first-time violations that exceed minor disruption, or any Tier 1 repetition after staff correction. Formal warnings expire after 6 months without further violations.

- Persistent off-topic posting after a Tier 1 correction
- Targeted rudeness or dismissive behaviour toward any member
- Non-English in English-only channels after staff redirection
- Uncorrected profile or nickname violations after a staff request
- Unauthorised staff or role pings
- First instance of mini-modding without staff authority
- Bot or voice misuse after a Tier 1 correction
- Continuing a behaviour after a direct staff instruction to stop

**Outcome:** formal warning logged with 6-month expiry, DM sent. Two active warnings trigger a Tier 3 review; three or more accelerate all future escalations.

### 6.3 Tier 3 — Temporary Mute (30 minutes – 24 hours)

Issued for persistent misconduct, deliberate defiance of staff, or behaviour actively disruptive to the community.

- Persistent toxicity or aggression after a Tier 2 warning
- Deliberate targeted trolling or repeated provocation
- Sexual innuendo or boundary violations directed at any member
- Voice channel disruption after staff correction
- Direct defiance of any staff instruction
- AutoMod bypass attempts (character substitution, zero-width characters, misspellings)
- Mini-modding after a formal Tier 2 warning
- DMing staff to dispute or pressure an active moderation action
- Refusing a nickname change after a staff request
- Publicly venting about a moderation decision instead of appealing

**During a mute:** you retain read-only access — no sending, reacting, voice, or bot interaction. You receive a DM with your case number, reason, and duration. The mute period is not negotiable after issuance.

**Mute evasion — zero tolerance:**
- Relaying messages through a friend, alt, or third party → mute duration **doubled**
- Using reactions, bot interactions, or indirect mentions to communicate → mute duration **doubled**
- Rejoining on an alternate account during an active mute → **immediate permanent ban of all associated accounts**, reported to Discord Trust & Safety. No warning. No appeal.

**Mute duration scale:**
```
First mute — minor offence        →  30 minutes
First mute — moderate offence     →  1 to 3 hours
First mute — severe offence       →  6 to 12 hours
Second mute (any severity)        →  12 to 24 hours
Third mute                        →  Automatic escalation to Tier 4
```

### 6.4 Tier 4 — Temporary Ban (1–30 days)

Issued for severe or sustained misconduct, repeated failure to correct behaviour after prior sanctions, or actions causing direct harm to the community.

- Three or more mutes with no sustained behavioural improvement
- Malicious impersonation of staff, a bot (Noxx, Cattito, Xyron), or another member
- Doxxing threats, dox teasing, or implying possession of private member information
- Sharing extremist-adjacent or hate-adjacent content after prior warnings
- Coordinating, participating in, or recruiting for a raid or mass harassment campaign
- Sustained cross-channel targeted harassment of a specific member
- Repeatedly undermining staff authority — public backtalk campaigns or proxy lobbying
- Providing false information to staff during an active moderation review
- Leaking server content — mod logs, staff discussions, ticket conversations, bot configs — externally

**Ban duration scale:**
```
First offence — moderate severity      →  1 to 3 days
First offence — high severity          →  7 to 14 days
Second temporary ban (any cause)       →  14 to 30 days
Third temporary ban                    →  Automatic Tier 5 — Permanent Ban
```

**Recidivism rule:** reoffending within 30 days of returning from any temporary ban is automatic escalation to the next tier, regardless of the new offence's severity.

### 6.5 Tier 5 — Permanent Ban

- Instant bans (zero-tolerance content), escalation bans (third temporary ban), and confirmed zero-tolerance violations.
- Appealable only for documented staff error.

### 6.6 Core Principles

- **6-month rolling record.** Warnings expire after 6 months without further violations. Only active warnings count toward escalation.
- **Ignorance is never a defence.** Enforcement proceeds regardless of whether you claim to have read the rules.
- **Impact determines severity.** Jokes, irony, sarcasm, and accidents are judged by their impact — not your intent.
- **First offenses get leniency.** A member with no prior record receives a warning or verbal correction for most first-time minor infractions.
- **Loophole attempts escalate penalties.** Any "grey area" argument, technicality claim, or "it doesn't say that specifically" response escalates to the highest tier available for that offence.
- **No second chances for zero-tolerance violations.** No account age, server standing, relationship, or personal circumstance changes this. Ever.
- **Staff decisions are final.** Disagreement is not grounds for escalation. The appeal system is.
